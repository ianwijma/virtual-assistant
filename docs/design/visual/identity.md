# Identity

The name, the feel, and what makes the product recognisable. Part of
[the visual design](README.md).

Status: started. The feel is set. The name is open.

## Design

- **The product is recognisable as ours,** on every device.
- **It feels calm, warm, precise and playful.** The interface is the calm
  and precise part: quiet, clean and fast, so that it suits a power user
  at a desktop. The assistants are the warm and playful part: the
  characters, their expressions and their voices bring the life, so that
  it suits a home. It is both a tool and a thing for the home.
- **The look is inspired by Apple's Liquid Glass, and is not a copy of
  it.** See [glass](glass.md). What sets it apart is a question below.
- **The name and the logo are chosen together with this work.** "Virtual
  Assistant" is a working title. Candidates are on
  [the name and logo board](examples/name-and-logo-board.html).

## What could make it ours

These are the candidates, shown on
[the direction board](examples/direction-board.html). They can be
combined.

| Candidate | What it means |
|---|---|
| The colour | An accent and a tint that Apple does not use |
| The backdrop | Our own soft, moving colour behind the glass, the same on every device |
| The assistants | The characters are the face of the product, and the interface around them stays quiet |
| An assistant's colour | The backdrop takes the colour of the assistant that is active, so the screen shows who is listening |
| The shape | A corner and a control shape that are our own |

## Open questions

- Which products do you admire the look of, besides Apple's?
- What should set the look apart from Apple's, so that it is recognisable
  as ours: the colour, the backdrop, the assistants, the shape, or
  something else? See the table above.
- Who is the design for first: the power user at the desktop, or the
  whole household at a tablet, guests and children included? When a dense
  screen and a friendly screen conflict, which wins?
- Which name? See the name and logo board. The names on it are not
  checked for existing products or trade marks.
- Which logo? "Glass pane with eyes" and "Speech drop" are to be worked
  out further. "House with a voice", "Hearth glow" and "Orbiting dots"
  stay in the running. "Overlapping panes" is dropped.
