# Voice page

How the look applies to the voice page. Part of
[the visual design](README.md). What the page does is in
[the technical design](../technical/clients/web-voice.md).

Status: started. The design follows the phone app.

## Design

- **The page reuses the design of the phone app.** Talking to an
  assistant is the same call: the figure has the screen, with the same
  two buttons, Chat and End, and the call folds into the chat in the
  same way. See [phone app](phone.md).
- **A guest can talk at once.** The two ways to set the page up, as a
  personal or as a household device, are offered without standing in a
  guest's way.
- **An old tablet's browser gets the simpler glass and reduced motion.**
  See [glass](glass.md) and [motion](motion.md).

## Open questions

- Where on the phone app's call design do the two set-up choices and
  the note for guests go? Ideas are on
