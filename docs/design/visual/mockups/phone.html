Heem on the phone, personal use

The phone as one person's device. Twenty-six screens, five to a row. The tab bar has four buttons the person sets (Overview, Juno, Timeline and Chat to begin with) and the fixed All button, with a grid icon and the word. Roomy density, touch sized. Icons are Phosphor, outlined. Where nothing is decided the screen is drawn plainly and its note says "Not decided".
