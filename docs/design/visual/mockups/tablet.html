Heem on the household tablet, kiosk

A tablet in a room, left running as a voice device for everyone. Landscape, roomy density, readable from across a room. Frames are drawn at about 590 by 400 so that two fit in a row; text sizes are scaled to match. The settings are drawn at a true 1024 by 700. Light and dark frames are mixed to show both. The figure is the stand-in with two eyes, and the wallpaper a stand-in for a photo.
